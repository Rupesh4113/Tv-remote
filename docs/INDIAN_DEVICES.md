# Indian TV & Set-Top Box Ecosystem Guide

## 1. DTH & Satellite Providers
India's satellite television landscape is driven by DTH (Direct-to-Home) providers:

| Provider | Protocol | Carrier | Customer Code | Notable Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tata Play** (formerly Tata Sky) | NEC / Specialized | 38 kHz | `0x00BF` | HD, Binge+ Android Box, Organizer/Guide |
| **Airtel Digital TV** | NEC | 38 kHz | `0x00FF` | HD & Xstream 4K Android Box |
| **Dish TV** | NEC | 38 kHz | `0x00FF` | DishNXT HD, SMRT Hub |
| **Videocon d2h** | NEC | 38 kHz | `0x00BF` | HD / 4K Box, Active services |
| **Sun Direct** | NEC | 38 kHz | `0x00FE` | HD DTH, regional South Indian channel mappings |
| **DD Free Dish** | NEC | 38 kHz | `0x00FF` | FTA MPEG-2/MPEG-4 satellite receivers (Solid, Melbon) |

## 2. Digital Cable MSOs
Regional and national Multiple System Operators (MSOs):
- **Hathway**: Cisco/Humax/Android Playbox cable receivers.
- **Den Networks**: Digital cable boxes across Northern & Western India.
- **GTPL (GTPL Hathway / KCBPL)**: Dominant cable provider in Gujarat, Maharashtra, West Bengal.
- **Siti Cable (Essel Group)**: Digital cable STBs.
- **Asianet**: Premier cable and broadband provider across Kerala.

## 3. Indian & Popular Domestic TV Brands
- **Tier 1 Smart Brands**: Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Sony Bravia (Google TV/Android TV).
- **Popular Indian / Value Smart Brands**: Xiaomi / Redmi (PatchWall), OnePlus TV (OxygenPlay), Vu Cinema TV, Realme, TCL, iFFALCON, Thomson, Kodak, Blaupunkt, Acer, Coocaa, Motorola, Sansui, Lloyd.
- **Legacy Indian Brands (IR Focus)**: Onida, Videocon, Micromax, Intex, BPL, Salora, Weston.
